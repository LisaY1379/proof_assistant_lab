Mathematical scope

Real A; independent, mean-zero subgaussian coordinates; t ≥ 0. V and L are positive in the displayed derivation. If A = 0 or K = 0, the centered form vanishes; t = 0 is immediate. Constants c,C are absolute. Toolkit results are dependencies, not elementary proofs.
