Let \(\|\cdot\|\) denote the operator norm and \(\|\cdot\|_F\) the Frobenius norm. We prove that for an absolute constant \(c>0\), \[ \mathbb P\!\left(\left|X^{T}AX-\mathbb E X^{T}AX\right|\ge t\right) \le 2\exp\!\left[ -c\min\left\{ \frac{t^{2}}{K^{4}\|A\|_{F}^{2}}, \frac{t}{K^{2}\|A\|} \right\}\right]. \]
We use the following standard consequences of subgaussianity.
1. If \(Y\) is mean-zero and \(\|Y\|_{\psi_2}\le K\), then \[ \mathbb E e^{sY}\le e^{Cs^{2}K^{2}},\qquad s\in\mathbb R. \] Consequently, for independent such \(Y_i\), \[ \mathbb E\exp\left(s\sum_i u_iY_i\right) \le \exp\left(Cs^{2}K^{2}\|u\|_2^{2}\right). \]
2. One has \[ \|Y^{2}-\mathbb EY^{2}\|_{\psi_1}\le CK^{2}. \] Thus Bernstein's inequality gives \[ \mathbb P\left(\left|\sum_i b_i(Y_i^2-\mathbb EY_i^2)\right|\ge t\right) \le 2\exp\left[-c\min\left\{ \frac{t^2}{K^4\sum_i b_i^2}, \frac{t}{K^2\max_i|b_i|} \right\}\right]. \tag{1} \]
All constants below are absolute and may change from line to line.
### 1. Reduction to the symmetric case
Set \[ B=\frac{A+A^T}{2}. \] Then \(X^TAX=X^TBX\), while \[ \|B\|\le \|A\|,\qquad \|B\|_F\le \|A\|_F. \] It therefore suffices to assume that \(A\) is symmetric.
Write \[ X^TAX-\mathbb EX^TAX=D+Z, \] where \[ D=\sum_i a_{ii}(X_i^2-\mathbb EX_i^2), \qquad Z=\sum_{i\ne j}a_{ij}X_iX_j. \] The off-diagonal terms have mean zero because the coordinates are independent and mean-zero.
### 2. The diagonal part
Applying (1) with \(b_i=a_{ii}\), and using \[ \sum_i a_{ii}^{2}\le \|A\|_F^{2}, \qquad \max_i|a_{ii}|\le \|A\|, \] we obtain \[ \mathbb P(|D|\ge t) \le 2\exp\left[-c\min\left\{ \frac{t^2}{K^4\|A\|_F^2}, \frac{t}{K^2\|A\|} \right\}\right]. \tag{2} \]
### 3. Decoupling the off-diagonal part

### 5. Chernoff's bound
